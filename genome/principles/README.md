# Forma principles

These principles guide human decisions. Their IDs, ownership, status, and source locations are recorded in the [registry](../registry.ts).

## Clarity before density

Make the user's next decision understandable before optimizing for how much information fits on screen.

When a workflow becomes crowded, first clarify what the user is deciding and what information supports that decision. Evaluate density in context rather than assuming fewer visible elements always improve comprehension.

## Hierarchy communicates intent

Visual emphasis should reflect the importance and consequence of an action or piece of information, not decoration.

Review prominence against the decision being made. Consequence and forward progress are different considerations; making an action destructive does not by itself determine every aspect of its hierarchy.

## Reveal complexity progressively

Keep common workflows focused and introduce advanced controls when they are relevant rather than exposing everything simultaneously.

Progressive disclosure should preserve discoverability and access to information needed for the current decision. Human review determines when an advanced control is relevant.
